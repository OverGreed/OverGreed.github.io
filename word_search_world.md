# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Word Search World is a puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored on your device or processed when you use the app.

## Information stored on your device

The app may store limited local data on your device to keep the game working correctly, including:

- game progress and unlocked content;
- hint balances and gameplay state;
- notification preferences;
- local purchase state used to remember whether ads were removed on this device;
- basic app settings required for the user experience.

This local data is stored on the device and is not sold by the app developer.

## Advertising

The app may display ads through Google AdMob on supported mobile builds.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, performance data, and ad interaction data in order to deliver, measure, and improve advertising.

Learn more:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## In-app purchases

If you buy Remove Ads or other in-app items, payment processing is handled by the platform store provider, such as Google Play or the App Store.

The app developer does not receive your full payment card details.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app stores your notification preference and may schedule local reminders on your device.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the app settings or in the device settings.

## Data sharing

The app does not sell your personal data.

Data may be shared only with service providers that are necessary to operate app functionality, such as:

- Google AdMob for ads;
- Apple App Store or Google Play for purchases;
- platform notification services for local notification delivery.

## Your choices

You can:

- disable notifications in the app settings;
- purchase the ad removal option where available;
- clear local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage through the device settings.

## Children

If you believe a child is using the app and you have a privacy concern, use the contact details provided in the store listing for the app.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version should be kept with the current app release materials.
